Two ways to play

Desktop or console mode.

Same library, same settings — pick the experience that fits the moment.

Desktop mode

Use Jolven as a regular app inside GNOME, KDE or any Linux desktop.

Jolven Session

Start straight into Jolven from your display manager for a temporary console-like experience — no desktop loaded.

1

Install Jolven on your existing Linux distribution.

2

Optionally enable the Jolven Session for your login screen.

3

Grab the controller. Leaving the session returns you to the display manager.

Not a distro, not an OS

Your Linux stays your Linux.

Jolven doesn't replace your distribution. Keep Fedora, Ubuntu, Arch — whatever Linux you already use — and turn your PC into a console whenever you want. Jolven complements your Linux; it never competes with it.

Get Jolven.

On Fedora, one command downloads and installs Jolven for your user.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zonaro/jolven/main/install.sh | bash

The installer downloads the official binary, handles dependencies and installs everything for your user. Then open Jolven from your app menu.

FAQ

Questions before pressing Play?

Is Jolven a Linux distribution?

No. Jolven runs on top of your existing Linux installation — Fedora, Ubuntu, Arch, whatever you use. It is a gaming interface and an optional dedicated session, not an OS.

Do I need Steam?

No. Jolven imports from Steam, Heroic, Lutris, Bottles, Flatpak, desktop entries, emulators and manually added games. Use any combination you like — or none at all.

Can I use Jolven without the dedicated session?

Yes. Jolven works as a regular desktop application inside GNOME, KDE or another graphical environment.

Can I return to GNOME or KDE?

Yes. The dedicated Jolven Session is optional. Leaving it brings you back to the display manager, where your usual desktop is untouched.

Will I lose my library when upgrading from the Cartridges fork?

No. On first launch, Jolven copies your existing library, covers, settings and metadata to its own folders. Your original data is never deleted.

Which systems are supported?

The app targets Linux desktops. Dedicated Jolven Session installation is currently prepared for Fedora.
